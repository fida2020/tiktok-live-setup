Battle Final Countdown

Enter each guest with their current points, pick a mode, then start the 10s countdown. All-at-once: everyone counted together, highest points wins outright. Elimination: lowest points drops each round (with a distinct "Eliminated" animation) - run it again for the remaining guests until 2 are left, and that final round also announces the winner automatically.

Box / Guest Battle Winner (manual trigger - real, used live)

TikTok LIVE doesn't expose a reliable automatic box-battle-result event anywhere yet, so trigger this yourself the moment you see the result on your own screen. Shows a full-screen 10s celebration with their photo and name, and automatically adds them to config/vipList.json so they get the VIP welcome banner if they join again.
